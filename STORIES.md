# Tourbo: what it is, who it's for, and the stories it must pass

**Tourbo is a tour manager for acts too small for a booking agency.** Tell it who you are, where you want to play and how many nights. It finds where your fans actually live (Qloo), picks rooms, works out the money for each night, routes the tour, writes your show notes and handles the venue emails.

## Who does what (README and internal wording; the UI speaks as Tourbo and says "fan data" and "your AI assistant", naming Qloo and Claude only on /connect)
- **Qloo** knows taste: where the act's fans cluster, which rooms and local acts those fans like, what each city loves, which local brands fit as sponsors.
- **Tourbo MCP server** (`packages/mcp`) turns Qloo into tour tools: score cities, find rooms, openers, show economics, routing, promo split, offer assessment, plan critic. It does no AI thinking.
- **Claude** is the agent. On the website it calls the MCP tools, decides which cities and deals are worth it, writes show prep and venue emails, and negotiates. In your own Claude Code or Desktop, *your* Claude calls the same tools.
- **You** approve: nothing is sent to a venue without a click, and every number is labelled as an estimate.

## Stories (each one has a test in `tests/stories/`)
1. **Maya, indie singer in Chicago (draw ~300).** She wants 5 shows within a day's drive in March that make money. She types her name, clicks Chicago on the map, sets 600 km and 5 shows, and presses Plan. She watches Tourbo work through clear steps and gets a map route, one card per night (room, opener, deal, ticket price, expected profit) and a tour total. *Pass:* every city is inside her circle, every night has a money verdict, a loss-making night is never presented as a headline.
2. **Maya preps a night and books it.** On a night's card she opens Show prep and gets an opening line, setlist nods to local acts, banter and a sponsor ask, each tied to a Qloo fact; nothing made up gets through. She sends the venue inquiries; a venue offers a $300 flat fee, and Tourbo shows it loses money and drafts a counter-offer for her to approve.
3. **Rohan, stand-up comic in Mumbai.** He wants 6 shows around Mumbai and Pune in comedy rooms. *Pass:* he gets comedy clubs or theatres (no stadiums), fellow comics as openers, prices in ₹, and local hooks for his material.
4. **Ana, a new act not on Qloo.** Her name isn't found, so the form asks "Who do you sound like?" and she adds two acts. *Pass:* the plan is built from those acts' fans and says so plainly.
5. **Dev, who lives in Claude Code.** He clones the repo, sets his Qloo key in `.env`, opens Claude Code in the folder (the project's `.mcp.json` registers Tourbo) and asks for "a 4-show tour for Japanese Breakfast near Seattle". *Pass:* Tourbo's tools are listed, and his Claude plans with them.
6. **Lee, booker at a small Chicago bar.** He has empty Tuesdays. He enters his venue and gets acts his room's crowd likes that are small enough to book and have local fans.
7. **Things go wrong.** The act isn't found, Qloo is slow, the AI key is missing, the database is down, a run dies mid-way. *Pass:* each case gets one plain sentence saying what happened and what to do, plus a retry where it makes sense. Never a stack trace or a blank page.
8. *(Full run with Claude: story 1 end to end on the live app, `STORY_LLM=1`.)*
9. **Mira, a painter not on Qloo.** She asks her own Claude (MCP) for a European gallery run, compared to Banksy. *Pass:* `tourbo_disciplines` lists her discipline; spaces are galleries and museums (never clubs); stays are about two weeks per city; money counts print and original sales per visitor, not just entry. The same tools and agent serve singers, comics, DJs, dancers, poets, authors and podcasters; the hosted site offers the disciplines in `DEMO_DISCIPLINES`.
10. **Maya keeps her tour in one place.** She makes a project ("Spring tour 2027"), starts a plan from it (or adds an existing plan from the plan page), and keeps to-dos, setlist ideas and city notes there. Her own Claude reads and adds to the same project (`tourbo_project_*`) and gets it back as markdown she can paste into Notion. *Pass:* notes and to-dos save and come back; a to-do ticks done; deleting one item leaves the rest; an attached plan shows its cities and a link to its show notes; a bad project id gets a plain hint.
11. **Maya tours anywhere.** She draws a circle around a city that was never on a list (Boise, Lucknow, Porto, Nairobi, Medellín, Perth). *Pass:* she gets at least as many candidate cities as shows, all inside the circle, suburbs merged into their city, and a city brief for the town she clicks; a circle over open sea gets one plain "no cities here" sentence.
12. **Maya rehearses a night.** On the show-notes page she presses "Rehearse with fans": four simulated audience members built from that city's Qloo data (the superfan, the scene regular, the born-and-raised local, the plus-one) react to her notes; each line shows how many loved it or winced. *Pass:* every persona comes only from that city's brief and differs by city; a reaction that can't point at its persona's data is thrown out; it's labelled a rehearsal, not a prediction. Her own Claude gets the same panel from `tourbo_rehearsal_panel` and role-plays it.
13. **Does it pick what bookers picked?** Eight real 2024–25 tours of mid-size acts (public sources). Tourbo's top cities by fan score vs the real stops, next to Claude alone and a random pick. *Pass:* the Qloo column stays at or above the recorded mean and well above chance; the README table matches `backtest.json`.
14. **A judge lands on the home page.** The page shows real decisions Tourbo made for acts people know (a route that pays, a counter-offer, an opening line, a rehearsal), one at a time on a slowly turning globe whose fan terrain rises where the act is strongest; the top city (and a surprise city) is named on the globe. They can hover a marker, step Prev/Next, pick from "All" or pause. *Pass:* ≥ 50 examples on ≥ 6 continents, each complete and honest; the page reads only static files; hovering 40 px from a marker opens it without moving the camera; Next walks to neighbours the short way; reduced motion never auto-advances; it works at 390 px by touch; the perf gate (`npm run perf:home`) holds.
15. **Every screen looks like one calm product.** Tokens give one strong accent and AA contrast in both modes; two fonts, no monospace, no glow; no hard-coded colours in components; home, wizard and connect render with no console errors.
17. **Tourbo speaks as Tourbo.** Home, wizard, projects, a plan and its show notes never name the fan-data or AI vendor; `/connect` (setup) does, and lists at least four assistants.
